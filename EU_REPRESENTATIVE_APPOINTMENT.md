## 2. Appointment

The Company hereby appoints the Representative as its representative in the European Union for the purposes of Article 27 GDPR.

The Representative accepts the appointment and confirms that they are resident in a Member State of the European Union (Federal Republic of Germany) and are able to act in that capacity.

---

## 3. Scope of the Representative's mandate

The Representative is mandated to act on behalf of the Company in relation to its GDPR obligations, including but not limited to:

1. Acting as a point of contact for supervisory authorities and data subjects in the European Union on all issues related to processing of personal data, in accordance with Article 27(4) GDPR.
2. Receiving and forwarding to the Company any data subject requests (Articles 15–22 GDPR) addressed to the Representative.
3. Receiving correspondence from EU data protection supervisory authorities and forwarding it to the Company without undue delay.
4. Maintaining, jointly with the Company, the record of processing activities required under Article 30 GDPR insofar as it relates to processing covered by the Representative's mandate.

The Representative acts as a passthrough point of contact. Decisions concerning the processing of personal data, the implementation of technical and organisational measures, and the substance of responses to data subject and supervisory authority requests remain with the Company.

---

## 4. Liability and indemnification

In accordance with Article 27(5) GDPR, the designation of the Representative is without prejudice to legal actions which could be initiated against the Controller or the Processor themselves. The Company remains the entity responsible for compliance with the GDPR and shall indemnify the Representative against any cost, claim, or penalty arising from compliant fulfilment of the duties described in Section 3.

The Representative is not liable for the substantive content of the Company's processing decisions or for any infringement of the GDPR by the Company.

---

## 5. Term and termination

This appointment takes effect on the date stated at the top of this document and continues until terminated in writing by either party with thirty (30) days' written notice, or immediately on the occurrence of any of the following:

a) the Representative ceases to be resident in a Member State of the European Union;
b) the Company appoints a successor representative;
c) the Company ceases to engage in processing activities to which Article 27 GDPR applies.

Upon termination, the Company shall promptly update its Privacy Policy and any other public-facing materials to remove or replace the Representative's details.

---

## 6. Remuneration

The Representative serves in this role without remuneration. No fee, salary, expense reimbursement, or other consideration is paid by the Company to the Representative in connection with this appointment. The parties confirm that this arrangement reflects a family relationship and personal commitment, not a commercial service.
